# ContentCrew Setup Guide

## What You're Getting
ContentCrew is an AI-powered content generation system with templates for SEO blog posts, social media content, and email newsletters.

## What's Included
- **SEO Blog Engine** — Research prompts, outline builder, full article writer
- **Social Media Engine** — Content pillars, repurposing, platform-specific copy
- **Email Engine** — Newsletter structure, subject lines, CTAs
- **Style Templates** — Adapt tone for your brand

## Quick Setup (45 minutes)

### Step 1: Define Your Brand Voice
Create a brief style guide:
- Tone (professional, casual, technical, witty)
- Vocabulary (words to use, words to avoid)
- Audience description (who reads your content)
- Content formats you publish

### Step 2: Set Up Your Content Calendar
Plan 30 days of content:
- 8 blog posts (2/week)
- 20 social posts (5/week across platforms)
- 4 newsletters (1/week)

### Step 3: Configure Your Platform
**ChatGPT Custom GPT:**
1. Create a new GPT for each content type OR one master GPT
2. Paste relevant prompts
3. Configure tone instructions
4. Add your brand guidelines as uploaded files

**Claude Project:**
1. Create one Project per content type
2. Add prompts as project artifacts
3. Set custom instructions for output format

### Step 4: Test Pipeline
1. Generate 1 blog post → review quality → adjust prompts
2. Repurpose into 3 social posts → check consistency
3. Create 1 newsletter → verify links and formatting
4. Finalize your workflow

## Workflow Suggestion
- Mondays: Generate 2 blog outlines (SEO Engine)
- Tuesdays: Write full blog posts (Blog Writer)
- Wednesdays: Repurpose into social + email (Social + Email Engines)
- Thursdays: Schedule and publish
- Fridays: Review performance, iterate prompts
