FAQ

Common Questions

How many emails will a client receive?

At most four, at days 1, 7, 14, and 30 past due. At day 60 the automation stops and hands the invoice to you for a human decision.

What happens when an invoice is paid?

You mark the status in the sheet and the sequence stops immediately — no further reminders are sent.

Can I change the timing?

Yes. The day thresholds are plain filter conditions in the scenario; the setup guide shows exactly where to edit them.

Do I need accounting software?

No. A Google Sheet is enough. The schema is deliberately simple so it works alongside whatever you already use.
