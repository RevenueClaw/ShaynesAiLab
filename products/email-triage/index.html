FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a paid Make.com plan?

No. The free tier covers 1,000 operations per month, which is enough for roughly 30–60 emails a day depending on your routing. You can upgrade later if you outgrow it.

Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. The included scenario has modules for both; you authorize whichever account you use during setup.

Will it delete or archive anything I need?

The spam route archives promotional and bulk mail. Every other category is labeled, routed, or escalated — nothing is silently deleted.

What happens when the AI is unsure?

Anything below the confidence threshold is escalated to a human instead of guessed at. That behavior is built into the scenario.
