FAQ

Common Questions

Which form builders work?

Any form that can POST to a webhook — Jotform, Typeform, Google Forms via a connector, or a custom HTML form. The setup guide covers the common cases.

Do I need a CRM?

No. A Google Sheet works out of the box and is the default path. CRM modules are included for HubSpot and Freshsales.

How are leads scored?

A rules-based BANT + fit score with clear weights. It is deliberately transparent so you can audit and adjust it, rather than a black-box model.

What happens to duplicates?

The scenario checks your tracker before writing. Duplicate email submissions on the same day are skipped rather than creating two records.
