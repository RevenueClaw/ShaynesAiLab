## Target Prospect Profile

1. **10–100 employee companies** (big enough to have ops pain, small enough to move fast)
2. **Operations-led founders, COOs, or office managers** — the person drowning in the "glue" work
3. **Already using Gmail + some ops tools** (Make/Zapier, Slack, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion) — shows they're not starting from zero
4. **High-repeat-task businesses:** agencies, consultancies, MSPs, property management, e-commerce ops, professional services, HVAC/plumbing/home-services franchises
5. **Pain signals:** hiring VAs, missing leads, slow invoice follow-up, support inbox backlog, manual meeting prep

## Outreach Channels (Ranked)

1. **LinkedIn DMs** — highest response rate for B2B services; send 10–15/day
2. **Email** — scale play; use Apollo.io or Hunter for contacts
3. **IndieHackers / Reddit** — answer questions, don't pitch; build authority
4. **Local business Facebook groups** — underpriced for finding service businesses
5. **Your own content** — long-term SEO; keep publishing weekly

## Daily Outreach Routine (30–45 min/day)

1. **Find 10 prospects** matching the profile (LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, local groups)
2. **Send 10 LinkedIn DMs** using Template 1 or 2
3. **Send 5 personalized emails** using Template 3
4. **Follow up** on unanswered messages from 2–3 days ago
5. **Post 1 piece of content** on LinkedIn or IndieHackers showing a real result or lesson

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already use Zapier/Make." | "Great — that's the plumbing. I build the AI layer on top: reading emails, drafting replies, qualifying leads, summarizing tickets. Automation + reasoning." |
| "We tried AI, it didn't work." | "Most failures are prompt + workflow fit issues. I start with a diagnostic and build exactly one workflow that's provable before anything else." |
| "We don't have budget." | "Start with the free diagnostic. If there's a clear win, we scope one small workflow first — often under $2,500 with payback in weeks." |
| "This sounds complex." | "The whole point is to make your life simpler. The first workflow is usually one task, one tool, one output." |
