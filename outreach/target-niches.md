# Target Niches for Shayne's AI Lab Services
*Built: 2026-10-15*

## Tier 1 — Highest Fit (Start Here)

These niches have the exact ops pain you solve, decision-makers who can buy quickly, and enough margin to afford $500–3,500/mo.

### 1. Marketing / Creative Agencies (10–50 employees)
**Decision-maker:** Owner, Operations Director
**Pain:**
- Inbox flooded with client requests, revisions, and feedback
- Slow lead response when inquiries come in after hours
- Manual reporting to clients every week
- Project updates scattered across email/Slack
**Best-first workflow:** Email triage + lead qualification
**Perfect-fit offer:** Growth tier ($1,500/mo) — content agent + email triage + lead routing

### 2. Property Management Companies (10–100 doors managed)
**Decision-maker:** Owner, Operations Manager
**Pain:**
- Tenant maintenance requests hit one inbox
- Late rent follow-up is awkward and inconsistent
- Vendor coordination is manual
- Move-in/out inspection reports take forever
**Best-first workflow:** Email triage + invoice/late-notice follow-up
**Perfect-fit offer:** Managed ($500/mo) → Growth ($1,500/mo)

### 3. MSPs & IT Service Providers (5–50 employees)
**Decision-maker:** Owner, Service Delivery Manager
**Pain:**
- Support tickets need triage before technicians see them
- After-hours alerts flood the team
- Client onboarding is repetitive
- Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) are manual
**Best-first workflow:** Support ticket summarization + meeting prep
**Perfect-fit offer:** Growth ($1,500/mo) → Enterprise ($3,500/mo)

### 4. E-commerce Operations (DTC brands, Amazon sellers)
**Decision-maker:** Owner, Operations Lead
**Pain:**
- Customer support emails spike during launches/holidays
- Inventory and supplier emails need triage
- Ad performance reports are manual
- Refund/return requests are repetitive
**Best-first workflow:** Email triage + support summarization
**Perfect-fit offer:** Managed ($500/mo) → Growth ($1,500/mo)

### 5. Professional Services (Accountants, Bookkeepers, Fractional CFOs)
**Decision-maker:** Firm owner, Practice Manager
**Pain:**
- Client documents and emails pile up during tax/deadline season
- Follow-up on missing documents is manual
- New client onboarding is clerical
- Invoice chasing is inconsistent
**Best-first workflow:** Invoice follow-up + document request automation
**Perfect-fit offer:** Managed ($500/mo) → Growth ($1,500/mo)

## Where to Find Prospects

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator Filters
- Company headcount: 11–100
- Job titles: "Owner", "Founder", "COO", "VP Operations", "Operations Manager", "Office Manager"
- Industries: Information Technology & Services, Marketing & Advertising, Real Estate, Financial Services
- Keywords in profile/company: "growing fast", "hiring", "virtual assistant", "operations", "agency"

### Directories & Lists
- Clutch.co — filter by industry, size, location
- G2/TrustRadius — find software companies with ops gaps
- Local Chamber of Commerce member directories
- Facebook groups: "Property Management Mastermind", "MSP Owners", "Agency Owners"
- IndieHackers — " Showcase" section and pain threads

### Low-Cost Data Sources
- Apollo.io: $50/mo for verified emails + LinkedIn data
- Hunter.io: email finder for domains
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator: $80/mo — worth it immediately

### Manual Signals to Look For
- Job postings for "Operations Manager", "Virtual Assistant", "Customer Support"
- "We're hiring" posts — usually means they're drowning
- Complaints about inbox/Slack in public posts
- Recent funding or growth announcements (need ops help)
