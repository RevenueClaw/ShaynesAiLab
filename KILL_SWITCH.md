# ShayneSAI Lab — Kill Switch Criteria

## When to pull the plug
If after 60 days of sustained effort this site isn't generating leads, kill it.

## Hard Kill Triggers (any one = kill)
1. **Zero email leads** after 14 days with >1,000 visitors/month
   - Current: ~141 visits/day = ~4,200/month
   - If >3,000 visitors in 30 days and 0 real leads → kill
   
2. **Zero affiliate clicks** after 30 days with >200 clicks on any link
   - Need a link tracker to measure this (TODO)
   - If Make.com clicks are zero for 30 days → kill
   
3. **Traffic declining** despite content growth
   - Currently declining: 278 → 58/day (80% drop in 5 days)
   - If <50 visits/day for 2 consecutive weeks → kill
   
4. **Cost exceeds revenue** by >$100/month for 3 months
   - Current costs: ~$0.10/day OpenRouter = ~$3/month
   - Domain: ~$12/year = ~$1/month
   - Total: ~$4/month
   - Need affiliate revenue to cover this (very low bar)

## Soft Kill Triggers (evaluate)
1. No improvement in traffic trend after 30 days of Phase 2 content engine
2. Zero nurture sequence progression (leads stuck at step 0)
3. Content quality isn't improving (still looks AI-generated)

## What to measure weekly
1. Traffic: visits/day, trend (up/down/flat)
2. Leads: new signups/week from starter-kit form
3. Affiliate clicks: Make.com referral dashboard (manual check)
4. Nurture: how many leads advanced to step 2+
5. Cost: OpenRouter spend ($/day)

## Measurement Tools
- Cloudflare Analytics for traffic
- leads.json for signups
- Make.com affiliate dashboard for clicks
- nurture logs for sequence progress
