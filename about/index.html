About

We Build and Run AI Operators for Real Businesses

Shayne's AI Lab designs, deploys, and manages AI operators that handle the repetitive operational work inside growing companies — email triage, lead qualification, support summarization, invoice follow-up, and more.

What Makes Us Different

Most AI agencies sell strategy decks. We sell working automations.

Every workflow we deploy has already been built, tested, and run in our own 3-node AI swarm before it ever reaches a client. We dogfood the exact systems we sell. If it breaks in our environment, we fix it before it touches yours.

  • Production-first: We deploy real agents connected to your email, CRM, calendar, and support tools — not ChatGPT wrappers.
  • Outcome pricing: Managed ($500/mo), Growth ($1,500/mo), and Enterprise ($3,500/mo) tiers built around hours saved and output delivered, not hours billed.
  • Human escalation built in: Every workflow routes edge cases and high-stakes decisions to a human. We don't automate judgment.
  • Continuous optimization: Monthly retraining, prompt tuning, and performance reporting as standard.

Who We Work With

We focus on service businesses, agencies, consultancies, MSPs, and professional-services firms with 10–100 employees — teams where one ops bottleneck can eat 20+ hours a week.

Typical first projects: email triage that sorts inbound requests automatically, lead qualification that routes hot prospects within minutes, or a support agent that drafts replies from your knowledge base.

How We Make Money

Revenue comes from managed AI operator services and pre-built agent templates. We also use affiliate links for tools we genuinely recommend.

  • Services: Monthly retainers and one-time strategy sessions.
  • AgentKit Templates: Self-serve AI agent blueprints for teams that want to build in-house.
  • Affiliate links: Some tool recommendations include referral links, at no extra cost to you.

We don't take sponsored placements or write paid reviews. If a tool has real weaknesses, we say so.

Why We Also Publish Free Content

Between client builds, we publish honest tool comparisons, automation guides, and free templates. It's our way of staying current, giving useful signal in a noisy market, and letting prospective clients see how we think before they buy.

The free resources are genuinely free. The starter kit, diagnostic, and guides have no hidden paywall.
