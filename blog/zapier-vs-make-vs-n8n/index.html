Zapier vs Make vs n8n: When to Use Each Automation Tool

September 20, 2026

Quick Verdict

Use Zapier when you need to connect two apps quickly with a pre-built integration and minimal setup. Use Make when your workflows involve multiple steps, conditional logic, and you want a visual builder without coding. Use n8n when you need self‑hosted control, custom JavaScript functions, or developer‑friendly features and are comfortable with some technical setup.

Overview

Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat), and n8n are the three most popular automation platforms for small teams. All three let you connect apps and automate repetitive tasks without writing code from scratch, but they differ significantly in complexity, pricing, and control.

  • Zapier – The easiest to use, with the largest app library (5,000+ integrations). Best for simple “if this, then that” workflows.
  • Make – A visual, scenario‑based builder that handles complex multi‑step automations. Offers more flexibility than Zapier at a lower price per operation.
  • n8n – An open‑source, self‑hosted platform. Gives you full control over data, custom code, and infrastructure. Ideal for teams with technical resources.

Price Comparison

Pricing is a major factor for ops teams at 1‑50 person companies. Here’s a realistic breakdown based on common usage:

  • Zapier – Free plan: 100 tasks/month, 5 Zaps, 15‑minute update time. Paid plans start at $19.99/month for 750 tasks, 20 Zaps, and 2‑minute updates. The $49/month plan gives 2,000 tasks. For heavy automation, costs escalate quickly.
  • Make – Free plan: 1,000 operations/month, 2 active scenarios, 15‑minute scheduling. Paid plans start at $9/month for 10,000 operations, 10 active scenarios, and 5‑minute scheduling. The $29/month plan offers 40,000 operations. Operations are cheaper per unit than Zapier tasks.
  • n8n – Self‑hosted: completely free (you pay only for your server). Cloud plans start at $20/month for 5,000 workflow executions, 5 active workflows, and 1‑minute polling. The $50/month plan gives 20,000 executions. Self‑hosted is the most cost‑effective if you have the infrastructure.

For a team automating 10,000 actions per month, Make ($9) is cheaper than Zapier ($49). n8n self‑hosted costs only your server time (often $5–$10/month on a small VPS).

Who Each Is For

Zapier

Best for non‑technical operations staff who need to connect two apps quickly. If your workflow is a simple “when a new lead comes in, add to CRM and send a Slack message,” Zapier is the fastest path. It’s also the safest choice when you need an integration for a niche app that only Zapier supports. The trade‑off is cost and limited logic.

Make

Ideal for power users who want more control without writing code. Make’s visual scenario builder handles loops, filters, and data transformations that would require multiple Zaps. It’s a good middle ground for operations teams that need to automate multi‑step processes like “process incoming invoices, validate data, update accounting software, and notify the finance team.” The lower per‑operation cost also makes it attractive for growing teams.

n8n

Built for teams with developer resources or a strong ops lead willing to learn. n8n shines when you need to keep data on‑premises, use custom JavaScript or Python functions, or integrate with internal APIs. It’s also the best choice if you want to avoid per‑task pricing and have the ability to maintain a server. Use n8n for complex, high‑volume automations where you need full control over execution and error handling.
