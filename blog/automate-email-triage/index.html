Automate Email Triage with Make.com and AI

August 25, 2026

Quick Verdict

Make.com combined with an AI model (like OpenAI GPT-4 or Claude) can handle 80% of your email triage automatically. It’s not perfect for complex legal or medical emails, but for support tickets, sales inquiries, and internal routing, it saves your ops team 3-5 hours per week. Budget $50-100/month for the stack.

Overview

Email triage is the bottleneck for small ops teams. A 15-person company receives 150-300 inbound emails daily. Some are urgent support tickets, some are spam, some are sales leads, and some are internal approvals. Manually sorting them is tedious and error-prone.

Make.com (formerly Integromat) is the glue. It watches your shared inbox (Gmail, Outlook, or a helpdesk like Front), passes each email through an AI prompt, and routes the email to the right team or folder based on the AI’s classification. This is not sci-fi. It’s a simple API call pipeline that takes about 2 hours to build and test.

The core flow: Email arrives → Make triggers → Send email body to AI → Parse response → Route to Slack channel, ClickUp task, or label.

Building the Pipeline (Step-by-Step)

Here is the actual architecture we use for a 12-person SaaS ops team. You will need three things:

  • An email source (Gmail, Office 365, or IMAP connector in Make)
  • An AI service (OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API, or a local model via Ollama)
  • A destination (Slack channel, ClickUp task, or CRM like HubSpot)

Step 1: Create a new scenario in Make.com. Use the Gmail or Outlook module: "Watch Emails" with filter (only unread, only inbox).

Step 2: Add an HTTP module. Send a POST request to your AI provider’s chat completion endpoint. Your prompt should be structured like this:

Classify this email into one of: [support, sales, billing, internal, spam].
Respond with only the category word.
Email: {{email.body}}

Step 3: Use a router module in Make to split based on the AI response. Each route sends the email to a different destination.

  • Support → Create a task in ClickUp with priority, assignee, and due date.
  • Sales → Send a Slack message to #sales-inbound.
  • Billing → Forward to accounting@company.com and label in Gmail.
  • Spam → Mark as read and archive.
  • Internal → Forward to the relevant Slack DM.

Step 4: Add a human-in-the-loop check. For high-priority or ambiguous emails (AI confidence below 0.7), route to a manager's Slack for manual review instead of auto-processing.

Price Comparison

Here’s what you actually pay for a production-ready setup. All prices are for a 10-20 person ops environment (~1,000 emails/month):

Tool/Service Monthly Cost Notes
Make.com (Pro plan) $9-$29 10,000 operations/month. One email triage flow uses 2-3 operations per email.
OpenAI GPT-4o API $15-$30 ~$0.015 per email (shorter emails cost less). Pay-as-you-go.
Claude 3.5 Haiku API $8-$15 Cheaper and faster than GPT-4 for simple classification.
ClickUp (Business plan) $12/user/mo If you route support emails into ClickUp tasks. Skip if you only use Slack.
Fireflies.ai $10/user/mo Not needed for email triage, but useful if you also want AI meeting notes alongside your email workflow.
Total stack $30-$50/mo Plus any external email hosting fees.

Hidden cost: Testing time. Expect to spend 2-4 hours in the first week tuning your AI prompt and testing edge cases (replies, forwards, calendar invites).

Who This Is For

Best fit: Ops teams at 1-50 person companies that use Gmail or Office 365 and have a shared inbox. If you handle 50-300 emails per day and have at least one person who can set up a Make scenario (or willing to learn in 2 hours).

Good alternatives: If you already pay for a full helpdesk like Intercom or Zendesk, use their built-in triage rules first. Make.com adds value when those rules don't let you use AI (e.g., "forward if the email tone is frustrated" is impossible with rules but trivial with AI).

Not ideal for: Regulated industries needing audit trails of every AI decision. Also not great if your emails are highly confidential and you cannot send them to a third-party API (though you can self-host a local AI like Llama if you have the hardware).

Make.com vs Alternatives

Here is a honest comparison of the main automation tools for email triage:

Tool Strengths Weaknesses
Make.com Flexible HTTP module, low cost per operation, excellent email connectors (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP, Office 365). No built-in AI agent memory; needs external API calls. Can be slow for real-time (30-second delay common).
Zapier (with OpenAI) Easier setup, better for non-technical users. Handles structured AI parsing with "Zapier's AI" built-in. Costs 3x more at scale ($30/month for 750 tasks vs Make's $9 for 10k ops). Less customizable.
n8n (self-hosted) Free if you have a server. Full control over data privacy. Can run local AI models. Requires DevOps knowledge. No official support. Setup time is 5-8 hours vs Make's 2 hours.

Our recommendation: Use Make.com unless you have a developer on staff who can maintain n8n. The time saved in setup alone covers the $9/month cost difference.
