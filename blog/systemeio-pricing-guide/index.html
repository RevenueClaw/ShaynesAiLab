Systeme.io Pricing: Is It Worth It for Ops Teams?

August 25, 2026

Quick Verdict

Systeme.io is a solid, budget-friendly all-in-one funnel builder for ops teams that don't need enterprise-grade automation depth. It beat Keap and ClickFunnels on price but falls short on native integrations and advanced workflow logic. Worth it if you're optimizing for cost and simplicity; skip it if your team relies on complex multi-step automations or CRM-heavy sales processes.

Overview

Systeme.io positions itself as an affordable all-in-one marketing platform combining landing pages, email automation, sales funnels, membership sites, and webinars. For ops teams at 1-50 person companies, the appeal is obvious: one tool instead of stitching together Mailchimp, Leadpages, and Teachable. But "all-in-one" often means compromises. Let's evaluate what you actually get at each tier and whether the tradeoffs matter for operations workflows.

What Systeme.io Does Well for Ops Teams

  • Pricing transparency — no hidden fees per contact or page view
  • Funnel builder with conditional triggers (e.g., "if purchased product A, move to sequence B")
  • A/B testing available on all plans
  • Membership site hosting included, not an add-on

Where It Falls Short

  • Limited native integrations — only ~20 direct connections
  • Automation logic is basic: no "wait until X date" or complex branching without workarounds
  • No native CRM — you'll need to export data or use Zapier/Make.com
  • Email deliverability can be inconsistent; many users rely on external SMTP

Cost per Contact Analysis

At $27 for 5,000 contacts, Systeme.io costs $0.0054/contact/month — roughly 5x cheaper than Mailchimp's comparable tier and 10x cheaper than HubSpot's Marketing Hub Starter. For ops teams managing large contact lists on tight budgets, this alone justifies consideration.

Head-to-Head: Systeme.io vs. Alternatives

vs. Keap (formerly Infusionsoft)

  • Price: Keap Pro starts at $129/month for 1,500 contacts. Systeme.io Enterprise at $97 handles 30,000 contacts.
  • Automation: Keap wins with native CRM, lead scoring, and appointment scheduling. Systeme.io lacks native calendar triggers.
  • Verdict for ops teams: Choose Keap if you need CRM-driven sequences (e.g., "score lead above 50, send sales task"). Choose Systeme.io if your funnel is mostly email + content delivery.

vs. ActiveCampaign

  • Price: ActiveCampaign Plus ($49/month for 1,000 contacts) vs. Systeme.io Webinar ($47 for 10,000 contacts). Systeme.io is cheaper at scale.
  • Automation: ActiveCampaign has conditional splits, predictive sending, and site tracking. Systeme.io's visual builder is simpler but weaker.
  • Verdict for ops teams: Use ActiveCampaign if your funnel requires behavior-based triggers (e.g., "visited pricing page 3 times, didn't buy"). Use Systeme.io if your needs are linear sequences and membership content delivery.

vs. Make.com (automation glue)

Systeme.io can integrate with Make.com to extend its automation capabilities. For example, you can trigger a Make scenario when a Systeme.io contact tags changes, then update a ClickUp task or log a meeting note in Fireflies.ai. This is a workaround, not a native feature, but it functionally covers many gaps for ops teams willing to invest setup time.

Who Each Plan Is For

Free Plan

Best for: Solo operators testing funnel automation without commitment. Ops teams should avoid — too limited for any real workflow.

Startup ($27)

Best for: Small teams (2-5 people) with simple lead capture → email sequence → membership site. Good entry point if you're currently paying separately for email + landing page tool.

Webinar ($47)

Best for: Teams running automated webinar funnels. The built-in webinar hosting eliminates need for Zoom Webinar or EverWebinar. Note: concurrent attendee limits apply (ask support for your region).

Enterprise ($97)

Best for: Growing ops teams managing multiple product funnels. The 30,000 contact limit handles most 1-50 person companies. The priority support response time is typically 4-6 hours versus 24+ on lower tiers.

Infinite ($297)

Best for: High-volume operations with complex membership structures. Only consider if you're hitting contact limits and need unlimited funnels — at this price, you're approaching ActiveCampaign or Keap territory where you get better automation for similar cost.
