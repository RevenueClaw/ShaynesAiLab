Quick Verdict

Make.com is the best value no‑code automation platform for ops teams at 1–50 person companies — more flexible than Zapier for the same price, but expect a steeper learning curve. For the five automations below, it saves roughly 8–12 hours per week once set up.

Overview

If you’re running operations for a small team, you’re probably drowning in repetitive tasks: triaging email, capturing leads, sending invoices, updating Slack, and syncing data between tools. Make.com (formerly Integromat) lets you automate these workflows without writing code. It’s not as beginner‑friendly as Zapier at first, but it offers more conditional logic, built‑in data transformation, and cheaper pricing for high‑volume scenarios.

This guide walks you through your first five automations step‑by‑step. Each one takes 15–30 minutes to build and will pay for itself in a week.

Head‑to‑Head: Make.com vs. Zapier vs. n8n

For small ops teams, the main alternatives are Make.com, Zapier, and self‑hosted n8n.

  • Make.com – Best price‑to‑power ratio. Offers routers, iterators, and aggregators out of the box. The visual builder is intuitive once you learn its quirks. Weakness: documentation can feel scattered, and error handling is less polished than Zapier’s.
  • Zapier – Most beginner‑friendly. Multi‑step Zaps are limited on free plans, and pricing jumps quickly at 2,000+ tasks/month. Good if you need to set up a simple automation in minutes and never touch it again.
  • n8n – Free and open‑source, but requires server hosting or paying for their cloud. You get infinite customisation, but ops teams without a dev resource usually find the setup overhead too high.

Verdict: For the five automations below, Make.com hits the sweet spot — affordable for 5,000–10,000 operations/month and flexible enough to handle conditional branching (e.g., “if email contains ‘urgent’ then alert Slack, else file in folder”).

Who Each Is For

  • Make.com – Ops leaders who have time to learn the platform and want to automate complex multi‑branch workflows without paying enterprise rates. Good if you already use Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack, and ClickUp or Fireflies.ai for meeting notes.
  • Zapier – Teams that value speed of setup above all else and have predictable, linear workflows (e.g., form submission → email notification). Avoid if you need conditional logic beyond “if/then.”
  • n8n – Tech‑savvy ops teams that want full control and zero per‑operation costs. Best for companies with a part‑time developer who can host and maintain it.
