Free CRM Options for Small Business in 2026

August 03, 2026

Quick Verdict

For most small teams, HubSpot’s free CRM is the best starting point, but Zoho offers more flexibility for a few users. If you need pipeline management without paying immediately, start with Freshsales.

Overview

Free CRM options in 2026 have matured enough that you can run a real sales pipeline without spending a dime — as long as you stay within user and contact limits. The trade-offs are usually in automation, reporting, and integrations. For ops leaders at 1–50 person companies, the question is not “should I pay?” but “which free tier gives me the most runway before I hit a hard wall?”

Here we compare three of the most practical free CRM tiers: HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Freshsales. All three offer a free plan that includes deal tracking, contact management, and basic pipeline views. We’ll look at where they shine and where they fall short.

Price Comparison

All three tools are free at the entry level, but paid plans kick in when you need more users, contacts, or features. Here’s a snapshot of the jump to the cheapest paid tier (as of early 2026):

  • HubSpot: Free (unlimited users, 1k contacts) → Starter at $45/month (billed annually) for 2 users, 5k contacts, and basic automation.
  • Zoho CRM: Free (3 users, 5k contacts) → Standard at $20/user/month (billed annually) for unlimited users, 50k contacts, and advanced automation.
  • Freshsales: Free (10 users, 2k contacts) → Growth at $9/user/month (billed annually) for unlimited contacts, email sequences, and phone integration.

If you’re a team of five, Freshsales free gives you immediate capacity. If you need more than 2,000 contacts, Zoho’s free tier is the most generous. HubSpot’s unlimited users on free plan is great for company-wide visibility, but you’ll hit the contact limit fast if you’re pushing leads through.

Who Each Is For

HubSpot Free CRM

  • Best for: Teams that want a simple, brandable pipeline and care about eventual scalability. Unlimited users means you can add sales, marketing, and support without extra cost.
  • Weakness: 1,000 contact limit, almost no automation, and paid add-ons for calling and sequences. You’ll feel the pressure to upgrade quickly if you’re actively prospecting.

Zoho CRM Free

  • Best for: Small teams (1–3 people) that need more contacts (5k) and a real CRM that can grow with custom fields, modules, and workflows. Good if you’re willing to learn Zoho’s interface.
  • Weakness: Only 3 users on free. The UI can be cluttered, and integrations with third-party tools (like Make.com) require API access, which is free but adds complexity.

Freshsales Free

  • Best for: Teams of 2–10 that want a modern, user-friendly CRM with built-in phone and email. The free tier is generous for a small sales team.
  • Weakness: 2,000 contact limit, and advanced features like sequences and AI lead scoring require paid plans. Pipeline customization is decent but not as deep as HubSpot or Zoho.

Further Reading

If you’re looking to extend your CRM stack without adding cost, consider these tools that integrate well with the free tiers above:

  • Automate workflows: Make.com – Connect your CRM to Slack, email, and other tools with no-code automation. Even the free plan covers basic integrations.
  • Project management: ClickUp – Use it alongside your CRM to track tasks, documentation, and customer projects. Free tier is generous for small teams.
  • Meeting notes & transcription: Fireflies.ai – Automatically capture and summarize sales calls. Integrates with HubSpot, Zoho, and Freshsales (paid plans required for full sync).
