Free CRM Options for Small Business in 2026

August 03, 2026

Quick Verdict

HubSpot Free CRM is the best all-rounder for teams that need a clean pipeline manager with easy integrations. If you have more than two users and want to avoid paying for basic features, Zoho CRM Free is the better value.

Overview

In 2026, the “freemium” CRM market is crowded. Most tools offer a usable free tier, but the differences in limits and features can make or break a small ops team’s workflow. For companies with 1–50 people, the goal is simple: track leads, manage deals, and collaborate without a per-user fee that eats your budget.

I looked at four popular free-tier CRMs – HubSpot, Zoho, Freshsales, and Bitrix24 – focusing on pipeline management, user limits, storage, and the hidden costs of outgrowing the free plan. Below is a real-world breakdown.

Who Each Is For

  • HubSpot Free CRM – Best for teams of 1–2 that need a clean, well-integrated CRM and plan to upgrade later. Excellent for inbound marketing users who already use HubSpot’s other free tools.
  • Zoho CRM Free – Best for teams of up to 3 that need customisation and don’t want to hit a wall at 2 users. Good if you need free email support.
  • Freshsales Free – Best for small sales teams that want built-in phone and email without extra tools. The free tier is generous for 3 users.
  • Bitrix24 Free – Best for teams that want an all-in-one collaboration platform (tasks, chat, files) plus CRM. Good if you have more than 3 users and don’t need deep pipeline features.
