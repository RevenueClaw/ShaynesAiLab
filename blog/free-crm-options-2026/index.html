Free CRM Options for Small Business in 2026

August 21, 2026

Affiliate Disclosure: Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you click and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only recommend tools I have tested and use myself.

Quick Verdict

For most small teams (1-50 people) that need actual pipeline management without enterprise pricing in 2026, HubSpot's free CRM or Zoho CRM Free remain the best balance of functionality and cost. Freshsales is a close third if you prefer modern UX. Avoid the "free forever" traps that limit you to 10 contacts; look for platforms that give you at least 500 contacts and deal tracking.

Overview

Free CRM options have improved significantly. In 2026, the line between "free" and "paid" is mostly about storage limits, automation, and integrations. If you need to track leads through a pipeline, log calls and emails, and assign deals to team members without spending money, you have real options. But if you need custom reporting, advanced automation, or anything beyond basic deal stages, you will need to pay. Here is what the market looks like right now.

Head-to-Head: HubSpot Free vs. Zoho CRM Free vs. Freshsales Free

HubSpot Free

Strengths: Unlimited users. Unlimited deals. Contact management up to 1,000 contacts. Includes email tracking, live chat, and meeting scheduling. The interface is clean and most operations leaders will find it intuitive. It integrates with Make.com for automation and Fireflies.ai for call transcription.

Weaknesses: Heavy marketing bias — HubSpot wants you to upgrade. Reporting is very basic. No deal rotation or territory management. If you hit 1,000 contacts, you either delete data or upgrade (starts at $20/user/month). Automated workflows are locked behind paid tiers.

Zoho CRM Free

Strengths: 3 users free. Up to 500,000 records (contacts, leads, deals, etc.). Includes field-level customization, workflow rules, and web forms. The best free option for teams that need to customize their pipeline stages. Integrates natively with Zoho's suite (email, projects, books). Also connects to Make.com for advanced workflows.

Weaknesses: Only 3 users on free plan. Interface feels cluttered and dated. Mobile app is mediocre. Setup takes more time than HubSpot. High tendency to upsell during onboarding.

Freshsales Free

Strengths: Unlimited users. Built-in phone and email (limited credits). AI-powered contact scoring (Freddy AI) even on free plan. Modern UI that is significantly better than Zoho. Includes visual pipeline management and custom deal stages. Works well with ClickUp for task management.

Weaknesses: Only 500 contacts on free plan. Limited storage (1 GB). No workflow automation on free. Phone credits run out quickly. The AI scoring can be noisy if you have bad data.

Who Each Is For

HubSpot Free

Best for teams that value simplicity and need multiple users on the same pipeline. If you are a 5-person sales team that wants to stop using spreadsheets, start here. The learning curve is short. You will outgrow it when you need automation or more than 1,000 contacts.

Zoho CRM Free

Good for small teams (up to 3 users) that need heavy customization. If you need to track multiple deal types, use custom fields extensively, or integrate with Zoho apps, this is your best free option. The contact limit is generous, but the user limit will force an upgrade if you grow.

Freshsales Free

Best for very small teams (1-5 people) that want a modern interface without paying. The AI scoring is actually useful for low-volume sales. The 500-contact limit is the main downside. If your pipeline stays under that, it is a strong choice.

Bitrix24 Free

Only consider if you need an all-in-one (CRM + project management + email marketing). It is clunky, and the free plan shows ads. But the functionality is surprisingly deep for zero dollars. Use only if HubSpot or Freshsales cannot meet your needs.
