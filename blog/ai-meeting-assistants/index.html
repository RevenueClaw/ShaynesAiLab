AI Meeting Assistants Compared: Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom

September 16, 2026

Disclosure: Some links below are affiliate links. If you purchase through them, Shayne's AI Lab may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we have tested or used in production environments.

Quick Verdict

For most ops teams under 50 people, Fireflies offers the best balance of transcription accuracy, CRM integration, and value. Fathom is the better choice if you live inside Zoom/Meet and want a free, lightweight layer. Otter is the most polished but gets expensive fast once you need more than 1,200 minutes a month.

Overview

Meeting assistants have moved from "nice to have" to "table stakes" for ops teams. The problem is not a lack of options—it's that most comparisons are written by people who tested the free trial for 20 minutes. This guide is based on 30 days of real usage across three tools, with a focus on what actually matters for ops: accuracy on technical terms, CRM/ClickUp integration depth, and whether the AI summaries are actually useful or just word soup.

All three tools record, transcribe, and summarize meetings. The differences are in the details: how they handle speaker identification, whether they can push action items to your project manager, and what happens when you have a 2-hour vendor demo with heavy jargon.

Head-to-Head

Otter.ai

Strengths: Best-in-class speaker recognition. Otter is the only one of the three that reliably differentiates between two people with similar voices. The live captioning is also the most accurate—if you have non-native English speakers on the call, Otter will transcribe them more cleanly than the others. The UI is the most polished, and the "Otter Assistant" can join meetings automatically.

Weaknesses: The free tier is a trap. You get 300 minutes per month, which sounds like a lot until you're doing 5 hours of vendor calls a week. The paid plans jump to $16.99/user/month (billed annually), and that's for the Pro tier. The AI summaries are decent but not dramatically better than Fireflies' free version. Also, Otter's native integrations are limited—you'll need Zapier or Make.com to get data into most ops tools.

Fireflies.ai

Strengths: The most flexible in terms of where it works—it handles Zoom, Meet, Teams, and even audio files uploaded after the fact. The free plan gives you unlimited transcription (capped at 30 minutes per meeting), which is generous. The AI summaries are actually good at extracting action items, not just summarizing. The CRM integrations are the deepest of the three—Salesforce, HubSpot, and ClickUp all work natively.

Weaknesses: The UI is functional but dated. The "conversation intelligence" features (tracking talk time, sentiment) are gimmicky and rarely useful for ops. The transcription accuracy on technical terms is slightly behind Otter—expect to correct acronyms like "API" or "SLA" manually. The mobile app is also the weakest of the three.

Fathom

Strengths: The free plan is genuinely unlimited—no minutes cap, no meeting length cap. That's rare. Fathom's summaries are the most concise and actionable of the three. It doesn't try to transcribe everything; it focuses on decisions, action items, and owners. The Zoom/Meet integration is native and lightweight—no bot to invite, it just works. For a small team, you can get 90% of the value for $0.

Weaknesses: Fathom is Zoom/Meet only. No Teams support, no phone call recording, no audio file upload. The free plan is so good that you'll outgrow it quickly if you need advanced features like custom vocabularies or multi-team workspaces. The paid tier is $15/user/month, which is fine, but you're paying for essentially the same features Fireflies gives you free.

Who Each Is For

Choose Otter if:

  • You have frequent meetings with non-native English speakers and need the most accurate live captions.
  • You want the most polished UI and don't mind paying for it.
  • You need a tool that works well on mobile for quick review.

Choose Fireflies if:

  • You use multiple platforms (Zoom, Meet, Teams) and want one tool for all.
  • You need deep CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot) or want to push action items to ClickUp natively.
  • You want a free tier that's actually usable for a small team.

Choose Fathom if:

  • You're a small team (under 10 people) and want a free tool that just works.
  • You primarily use Zoom or Google Meet and don't need CRM integration.
  • You prefer concise summaries over full transcripts.

Quick Verdict

There's no single "best" tool—it depends on your stack. But here's the practical breakdown:

  • Worst-kept secret: Fathom is the best free tool. If you're a 5-person ops team, start here.
  • Best value for growth: Fireflies.ai at $10/user/month gives you the most flexibility and the deepest integrations. The 30-minute free meeting cap is annoying, but the paid tier is reasonable.
  • Skip Otter unless: you have a specific need for its captioning accuracy or you're already in the Otter ecosystem. It's not 70% better than Fireflies, but it's 70% more expensive.

One more thing: none of these tools will magically fix your meeting culture. They'll save you from taking notes, but you still need to decide which meetings are worth having. Use the AI summaries to identify recurring meetings that could have been an email—your calendar will thank you.
