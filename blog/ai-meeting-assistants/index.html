AI Meeting Assistants Compared: Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom

August 16, 2026

Disclosure: Some links in this article are affiliate links. If you purchase through them, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. I only recommend tools I’ve tested and find genuinely useful for ops teams.

Quick Verdict

If you need deep CRM integration and automated action items, go with Fathom. For superior search across all meetings and team collaboration, pick Fireflies. If you want a simple, reliable transcript with decent AI summaries and don’t mind limited integrations, Otter works fine — but it’s the least flexible of the three.

Overview

Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Fathom are the three most popular AI meeting assistants for small teams. All three record, transcribe, and summarize meetings from Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. They also integrate with CRMs, project management tools, and automation platforms like Make.com and ClickUp. But their strengths differ — especially around search, action item extraction, and pricing for small teams.

Head-to-Head

Transcription Accuracy

All three tools use similar underlying speech-to-text models. In my tests, Otter and Fireflies are nearly identical in accuracy (around 95% for clear English). Fathom is slightly behind on heavy accents but catches up with manual edits. For most ops teams, the difference is negligible.

Search & Discovery

Fireflies wins here. Its “Ask Fred” AI lets you search across all meetings by keyword, speaker, date, or topic. You can find “when did we discuss the Q4 budget” in seconds. Otter has basic search but no cross-meeting AI querying. Fathom offers per-meeting search only — no global search across your library.

Action Items & CRM Sync

Fathom is the strongest. It automatically extracts action items, decisions, and follow-ups, and can push them directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, or ClickUp. Fireflies also extracts action items but requires manual review — the AI often misses context. Otter’s action item detection is basic and rarely usable without editing.

Integrations

Fireflies integrates with 50+ tools including Make.com, Slack, Notion, and Asana. Fathom has fewer native integrations but strong CRM and project management connectors. Otter has the smallest integration set — no Make.com or Zapier support on lower plans.

Meeting Bot vs. Personal Assistant

Otter and Fireflies use a bot that joins your calendar meetings automatically. Fathom uses a personal assistant that you invite manually (or via Chrome extension). The bot approach is more hands-off but can be intrusive to external guests. Fathom’s manual invite gives you more control but requires an extra click.

Who Each Is For

Otter.ai

Best for small teams that just need a simple transcript and summary without complex workflows. If you’re a 3-person startup that only records internal stand-ups, Otter’s free tier might be enough. But the meeting length cap (30 min on free, 90 min on Pro) is a dealbreaker for longer client calls or workshops. Avoid if you need CRM integration or cross-meeting search.

Fireflies.ai

Ideal for teams that attend many external meetings and need to quickly find past discussions. The unlimited free tier (with storage limits) is generous for testing. Fireflies’ “Ask Fred” search is genuinely useful for ops leaders who need to reference decisions from weeks ago. The $10 Pro plan is the cheapest paid option among the three. Downside: action item extraction is mediocre — you’ll still need to manually copy tasks into your project manager.

Fathom

Best for sales and customer success teams that live in a CRM. If you want meeting notes automatically turned into Salesforce opportunities or ClickUp tasks, Fathom does that better than the others. The free tier is surprisingly capable (unlimited recordings, basic AI). But the lack of global search across meetings is a real limitation for ops teams that need to audit historical conversations. Also, the manual invite process can be annoying if you have back-to-back meetings.
