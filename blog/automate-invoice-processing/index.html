How to Automate Invoice Processing with Jotform and Make

September 18, 2026

Quick Verdict

Jotform + Make is a solid, low-cost combo for automating invoice intake and approval routing. It works well for small teams that don’t need full AP software, but expect some manual setup and limited scalability beyond 100 invoices per month.

Overview

Invoice processing is a repetitive task that eats up hours in small ops teams. You receive PDFs via email, manually enter data into spreadsheets, chase approvals, and finally file everything. The goal is to cut that friction.

Using Jotform as the intake point and Make.com as the automation engine, you can build a workflow that:

  • Captures invoice details (vendor, amount, PO number, PDF upload) through a branded form.
  • Stores submissions in a Google Sheet, Airtable, or your accounting tool.
  • Sends approval requests to the right person based on amount thresholds.
  • Notifies the finance team when an invoice is approved or rejected.
  • Archives the PDF and data for audit trails.

No coding required. Both tools offer free tiers, so you can test the workflow before committing.
